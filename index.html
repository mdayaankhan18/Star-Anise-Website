The Star Anise experience

A patisserie, café and little escape in Colaba.

Star Anise brings together artisanal bakes, modern patisserie and gourmet café favourites in a warm, elegant setting. From flaky croissants and fresh sourdough to celebration cakes, entremets, sandwiches, coffee, matcha and more — there is something for every mood.

“A little luxury, for every day.”
